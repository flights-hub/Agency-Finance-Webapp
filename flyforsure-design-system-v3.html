§ 04 · Interactive states

The four states v2 forgot

Skeleton · empty · error · loading
Travel UX lives and dies on these states. "No flights found" runs on most obscure-route searches. Skeleton during fare-pull latency. Inline errors on the booking form. Tactile press for every CTA.
Loading

Skeleton card

Matches the real card's layout. Shimmer animates left-to-right. No generic spinners.

Empty

No flights match

Composed illustration, clear copy, suggested next action — never just "no results."

No flights for these dates

Try shifting your travel window by ±3 days, or check our charter inventory.

Error

Inline validation

Label above. Error below. Border tinted. No alert dialogs for field-level issues.

§ 07 · Principles

Seven, revised

Pinned to the wall
The taste-tuned principles for v3. Read them once. They're the test before any new screen ships.
P · 01

Divider before card

Group with border-t, divide-y, and whitespace. Cards only when elevation communicates hierarchy.

P · 02

Bento before grid

Equal columns are the AI tell. Make tiles asymmetric. One thing should be bigger, one wider, one wrapping below. Hierarchy by size, not by typography alone.

P · 03

Weight before scale

Control hierarchy with font weight (300 / 500 / 600) and color contrast, not by making H1 enormous. Premium systems whisper, they don't shout.

P · 04

Motion is a feature, not decoration

Every interactive element has a tactile :active. Lists stagger-reveal. The mesh gradient drifts. None of it asks for attention — all of it confirms the system is alive.

P · 05

Four states or it doesn't ship

No component is done until skeleton, empty, error, and tactile-press are designed. Especially "no flights found" — the most-trafficked state on the platform.

P · 06

Coral as accent, not theme

75% saturation. Reserved for accent moments — selected state, live indicator, featured pricing. Most CTAs are ink-900. Coral when it earns the spotlight.

P · 07

Concrete copy, not adjective stacks

"Book Delhi → Rome in 90 seconds" beats "Fast. Affordable. Hassle-Free." Specific verbs. Real routes. Real numbers. The brand voice is "we did it" not "we promise to."
